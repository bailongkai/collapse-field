塌缩带 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-09-13

《塌缩带》（下称"本游戏"）由个人开发者运营。本游戏不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱等可直接识别身份的信息。

本地存储

游戏进度（金币、解锁、成就、设置）保存在您的设备上，不会上传到我们的服务器。

广告

移动版使用 Google AdMob 展示激励视频与插页广告。AdMob 可能收集设备标识符与广告交互数据用于投放与统计，详见 Google 隐私政策。您可以在商城中购买"去除广告"停止插页广告。

内购

内购通过 App Store / Google Play 完成，并经由 RevenueCat 校验收据。我们不会接触您的支付信息。

分析

本游戏可能记录匿名的游戏事件（例如一局的时长与等级）以改进平衡，不含个人身份信息。

儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息。

Collapse Field Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-13

Collapse Field ("the game") is run by an individual developer. The game needs no account and collects no name, email or other directly identifying information.

Local storage

Progress (gold, unlocks, achievements, settings) is stored on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Advertising

The mobile version shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data; see the Google Privacy Policy. "Remove ads" in the store stops interstitials.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play and validated through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

Analytics

The game may record anonymous gameplay events (for example how long a run lasted) to improve balance. None of it identifies you.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
