# Credits

Collapse Field is made by one developer. This file says where everything that ships came from and
under what licence. A copy ships with the game as `CREDITS.txt`.

## Art

- **Characters, enemies, bosses, props, pickups, icons, decor and floors** (159 of the 181 frames in
  the game atlas) were generated by the developer with AI image models (FLUX, run on fal.ai) through
  `scripts/gen-art.mjs`, then cut out, scaled and tiled by `scripts/build-atlas.mjs` and
  `scripts/build-floor.mjs`. The prompts are in the script.
- **Interface bars, panels and particle sprites** (the other 22 frames: `bar_*`, `button_*`,
  `panel_*`, `fx_ring`, `p_flare`, `p_smoke`, `p_spark`) are by **Kenney** (https://www.kenney.nl),
  **CC0 1.0**. The tech-style button and panel frames are drawn by `scripts/build-atlas.mjs`.

## Sound and music

- **Sound effects** are by **Kenney**, **CC0 1.0**, from the packs listed below.
- **Music** is synthesised at runtime from the arrangement in `src/game/audio/score.ts`; no recording
  ships.

  https://github.com/atelier-anchor/smiley-sans. Copyright (c) 2022–2024 atelierAnchor, with Reserved
  Font Names "Smiley" and "得意黑". SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **CF Display** is a subset of **Orbitron** by The Orbitron Project Authors,
  https://github.com/theleagueof/orbitron. Copyright 2018 The Orbitron Project Authors, with Reserved
  Font Name "Orbitron". SIL Open Font License 1.1.

  Both are subset to the game's strings, which makes them Modified Versions under the OFL; that
  licence does not let a Modified Version use a Reserved Font Name, so they ship under the names
  above (`scripts/font-names.py`). The licence texts ship beside them in `assets/fonts/`.
- **Kenney Future** by **Kenney**, used for the digit font and as a fallback. **CC BY-SA 3.0**,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/. Used unmodified.
